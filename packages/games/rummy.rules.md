# Rummy

Collect your cards into sets and runs, then go out before anyone else. This is the 13-card style with jokers and a cut joker. Plainer versions are a few house rules away.

## Players and cards

- 2 to 6 players, each playing alone.
- One deck with its 2 jokers while that's enough cards; two decks (4 jokers) for bigger tables.
- Everyone gets 13 cards. The rest form the stock, face down, and one card is turned up to start the discard pile.
- **The cut joker:** after the deal, another card is turned face up and set aside. Every card of its rank is wild for this hand. If it's a printed joker, aces are wild. The set-aside card itself isn't played.

## Melds

- A **set** is 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, such as 7♣ 7♥ 7♠.
- A **run** is 3 or more cards of the same suit in order, such as 4♦ 5♦ 6♦. Aces are low (A-2-3).
- **Wild cards** (printed jokers and cut jokers) can stand in for any card in a meld.
- A **pure run** is a run with no wild card standing in for another card. You need a pure run before you can lay down any meld that uses a wild card, and before you can go out.

## Arranging your hand

You can group the cards in your hand into melds yourself at any time, even when it isn't your turn. Nobody else sees your groups. **Sort into melds** does it for you, finding the arrangement that leaves the fewest points.

## Play

Play goes to the left. On your turn:

- **Draw** the top card of the stock, or the top card of the discard pile.
- **Meld** (optional): lay down any sets or runs from your hand, and **lay off** cards onto melds already on the table (anyone's).
- **Discard** one card onto the discard pile. You can't discard the card you just took from the discard pile, unless it's your last card.

**Going out:** you go out when your hand is empty, either by melding all your cards or by discarding your last one.

**When the stock runs out,** the discard pile (all but its top card) is turned over to make a new stock, as often as needed.

## Scoring

When a player goes out, everyone else shows their hand arranged into melds. The player who went out scores the **deadwood** left in the other hands: the cards that aren't part of a meld, counted the way that leaves the fewest points.

- Kings, queens and jacks: 10
- Aces: 1
- Other cards: their number
- Printed jokers: 0

A player who has no pure run counts every card in their hand as deadwood.

**Going rummy:** if you go out by laying down your whole hand in one turn, without having melded anything before, your score for the hand is doubled.

## Winning

The first player to reach the target score (100 unless the table chose otherwise) wins.

## House rules

- **Play to:** 100 (the default), 150, 250 or 500.
- **Scoring:** "Winner scores the deadwood in other hands" (the default), or "Everyone scores their own deadwood; lowest wins".
- **Cards dealt:** 13 each (the default), 10 each, or Hoyle's "10 for two players, 7 for 3–4, 6 for 5–6".
- **Wild cards:** "Jokers and a cut joker" (the default), "Jokers only", or "No wild cards".
- **A pure run is needed** (on by default): turn it off and wild cards can be used freely, and nobody needs a pure run to go out or to have their melds count.
- **Melds:** "Laid down as you go" (the default), or "Kept in hand until you go out": nobody lays anything down during the hand. You go out by showing your whole hand arranged into melds as you discard your last card.
- **Aces in runs:** "Low (A-2-3)" (the default), "High (Q-K-A)", or "High or low" (but not K-A-2).
- **You must discard your last card to go out** (off by default): you can't go out by melding every card; you need one left to discard.
- **Going out all at once doubles your score** (on by default): the "going rummy" bonus above.
- **You may discard the card you just took from the discard pile** (off by default).
- **When the stock runs out:** "Turn the discard pile over and keep going" (the default); "Turn it over once, then end the hand"; or "End the hand". When a hand ends this way, everyone's hand is scored as usual.
- **Allow "Sort into melds"** (on by default): turn it off and players arrange their hands only by hand.
