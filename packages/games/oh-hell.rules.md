# Oh Hell

A trick-taking game where every trick counts, too few or too many. Bid exactly how many tricks you'll take, then take exactly that many. The hands grow and shrink as the game goes on.

## Players and cards

- 3 to 7 players, each playing alone.
- One standard deck. Aces are high.

## Hand sizes

The first hand is 1 card each, the next 2, and so on up to 10 cards, then back down to 1. With more players the biggest hand is smaller, so there is always at least one card left over to turn up for trump.

## Trump

After the deal, the next card is turned up. Its suit is trump for that hand. If no cards are left over, the hand is played with no trump.

## Bidding

Starting left of the dealer, each player bids once: how many tricks they will take, from 0 up to the number of cards in their hand.

**The hook:** the dealer bids last and may not make the bids add up to the number of tricks in the hand. That means at least one player will miss.

## Play

The player to the dealer's left leads the first trick. The winner of each trick leads the next.

- Follow the suit led if you can. If you can't, play any card, trump or not.
- The highest trump wins. If no trump was played, the highest card of the suit led wins.

## Scoring

- **Take exactly your bid:** 10 points plus your bid. A bid of 0 made is worth 10; a bid of 3 made is worth 13.
- **Take any other number:** nothing.

## Winning

After the last hand, the highest total wins. Players tied for the highest share the win.

## House rules

- **Hand sizes:** "Up then down (1 → most → 1)" (the default); "Down then up (most → 1 → most)"; "Up only (1 → most)"; or "Down only (most → 1)".
- **Most cards in a hand:** 10 (the default), 7, 13, or "As many as the deck allows".
- **The hook** (on by default): turn it off and the dealer may bid anything.
- **Scoring:** "10 + bid when exact, otherwise nothing" (the default); "1 per trick, plus 10 when exact", where every trick you take scores 1 point and making your bid exactly adds 10; or "10 + bid when exact, otherwise −1 per trick off", where missing your bid by 2 tricks, either way, loses 2 points.
- **Blind bids** (off by default): you may bid before looking at your cards. A blind bid that's made scores double.
- **The biggest hand is played with no trump** (off by default).
