# Canasta

A partnership rummy game played with two decks. Meld cards of the same rank, build canastas of seven or more, and go out.

## Players and cards

- 4 players in two partnerships (partners sit opposite), or 3 or 2 players each playing alone.
- Two standard decks and four jokers: 108 cards.
- Each player gets 11 cards with 4 players, 13 with 3, and 15 with 2.
- The rest form the stock. One card is turned up to start the discard pile. If it's a wild card or a red 3, another card covers it and the pile starts frozen.

## Special cards

- **Wild cards:** jokers and 2s. They can stand in for any rank in a meld.
- **Red 3s:** the moment you get one, it's laid down in front of you and you take a replacement card. Each is worth 100, or 800 for all four on one side. A side that never melded in the hand loses those points instead.
- **Black 3s:** block the discard pile when discarded (see below). They can only be melded when you're going out: three or four of them, with no wild cards.

## Melds

A meld is 3 or more cards of the same rank, with at least 2 natural cards and at most 3 wild cards. Partners share their side's melds and can add to them.

A meld of 7 or more cards is a **canasta**:

- **Natural canasta** (no wild cards): 500.
- **Mixed canasta** (with wild cards): 300.

**Your side's first meld:** the cards in the first melds your side lays down must add up to at least a minimum, which depends on your side's score: 50 normally, 15 when your score is below 0, 90 from 1,500, and 120 from 3,000. When taking the discard pile for a first meld, the top card counts toward this, but the other cards in the pile don't.

## A turn

- **Draw** one card from the stock, or **take the whole discard pile**. To take the pile you must meld its top card straight away, either with cards from your hand or onto your side's meld of that rank.
- **Meld** (optional): arrange your cards into groups (or press **Sort into melds**). Your side's first melds go down together with **Lay down my melds**. After that, meld group by group and add to your side's melds.
- **Discard** one card. You can't meld or add your last card unless you're going out: you need one to discard.

## The discard pile

- The pile is **frozen** for a side that hasn't melded yet, and for everyone once a wild card has been discarded onto it. A frozen pile can only be taken with two natural cards from your hand that match the top card. Taking it unfreezes it.
- Nobody can take the pile while a wild card or black 3 is on top.

## Going out

You go out by melding or discarding your last card. Your side needs at least one canasta first.

- **Going out:** 100.
- **Going out concealed** (melding your whole hand in one turn, having melded nothing before): 200. A concealed go-out doesn't need to meet the first-meld minimum.

**When the stock runs out,** play goes on while the player whose turn it is can take the discard pile, and they must. Otherwise the hand ends.

## Scoring

At the end of each hand, each side scores:

- Its canastas (500 natural, 300 mixed) and red 3s.
- The bonus for going out, if it went out.
- The value of every card in its melds: jokers 50; 2s and aces 20; K, Q, J, 10, 9 and 8 are 10; 7 down to 4 and black 3s are 5.
- Minus the value of every card left in its players' hands.

## Winning

The first side to reach 5,000 wins.

## House rules

- **Play to:** 5,000 (the default), 3,000 or 10,000.
- **Draw 2 cards from the stock** (off by default): take two cards instead of one when drawing. Two players always draw 2.
- **The discard pile is always frozen** (off by default): taking it always needs two natural cards matching the top card.
- **"May I go out?"** (off by default): before going out, you may ask your partner. If they say yes you must go out; if no, you can't go out this turn.
- **Going out concealed needs your partner to have melded** (off by default).
- **Wild cards in a meld:** "At most 3" (the default), or "Any number (still at least 2 natural cards)".
