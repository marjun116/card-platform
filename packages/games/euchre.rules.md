# Euchre

A fast partnership trick-taking game with a short deck. Your side names trump, and then has to take at least three of the five tricks.

## Players and cards

- 4 players in two partnerships. Partners sit opposite each other.
- 24 cards: the 9, 10, J, Q, K and A of each suit.
- Each player gets 5 cards. The other 4 form the **kitty**, and its top card is turned face up (the **upcard**).

## The bowers

Once trump is named, two jacks become the highest trumps:

- **Right bower:** the jack of the trump suit, the highest card.
- **Left bower:** the other jack of the same colour. It counts as a trump, not as its own suit. If hearts are trump, the J♦ is the left bower and counts as a heart.

Trumps rank: right bower, left bower, A, K, Q, 10, 9. The other suits rank A, K, Q, (J), 10, 9.

## Making trump

**First round:** starting left of the dealer, each player may **order up** the upcard, making its suit trump, or pass. If someone orders it up, the dealer takes the upcard into their hand and discards a card face down (any card but the upcard).

**Second round:** if everyone passed, the upcard is turned down. Starting left of the dealer, each player may now name any other suit as trump, or pass. If everyone passes again, the hand is thrown in and the deal moves on.

The side that named trump is the **makers**. The other side is the **defenders**.

**Going alone:** the player who named trump may choose to go alone. Their partner puts their cards down and sits out the hand.

## Play

The player to the dealer's left leads the first trick (skipping a partner who's sitting out). The winner of each trick leads the next.

- Follow the suit led if you can. Remember the left bower belongs to the trump suit.
- The highest trump wins. If no trump was played, the highest card of the suit led wins.

## Scoring

- Makers take 3 or 4 tricks: **1 point**.
- Makers take all 5 (a **march**): **2 points**.
- A lone player takes all 5: **4 points**.
- Makers take fewer than 3 (they're **euchred**): **2 points to the defenders**.

## Winning

The first side to reach 10 points (unless the table chose otherwise) wins.

## House rules

- **Play to:** 10 (the default), 5, 7 or 11.
- **Stick the dealer** (off by default): if the second round comes back to the dealer, the dealer must name trump. No hand is thrown in.
- **Euchring a lone player scores 4** (off by default): the defenders get 4 instead of 2.
- **A defender may go alone against a lone player** (off by default): one defender's partner sits out too. Euchring a lone player this way scores 4.
- **Ordering up your partner (the dealer) means going alone** (off by default): if you order up the upcard into your partner's hand, you must play alone.
- **Points when a lone player takes all five tricks:** 4 (the default) or 5.
