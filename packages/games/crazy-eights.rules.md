# Crazy Eights

A shedding game: be the first to get rid of all your cards by matching the card on top of the discard pile. Eights are wild.

## Players and cards

- 2 to 10 players, each playing alone.
- One standard deck. Two decks are shuffled together for 8 or more players, or when the cards dealt wouldn't leave enough of a stock.
- With 2 players each gets 7 cards; with more, 5 each.
- The rest of the cards form the stock, face down. The top card is turned up to start the discard pile. If it's an 8, it goes back into the stock and another card is turned up.

## Object

Be the first to play all your cards. The first player to reach the target score (100 unless the table chose otherwise) wins.

## Play

Play goes to the left. On your turn, play one card onto the discard pile that matches the top card's **suit** or **rank**. A 7♥ can go on any heart or any 7.

- **Eights are wild.** You can play an 8 on anything, and then you name the suit the next player must match. If an 8 is your last card, you don't name a suit.
- **If you can't play,** draw from the stock until you can, then play. You may also draw when you could play.
- **When the stock runs out,** it isn't refilled. A player who can't play passes.

## Scoring

When a player plays their last card, the hand ends and they score the cards left in everyone else's hands:

- Each 8: 50
- Each king, queen or jack: 10
- Each ace: 1
- Every other card: its number

**A blocked hand:** if the stock is empty and nobody can play, the player with the lowest hand wins it. They score the difference between their hand and each other hand. Players tied for lowest both score.

## Winning

The first player to reach the target score wins.

## House rules

- **Play to:** 100 (the default), 200, 500, or "One hand only".
- **Scoring:** "Winner scores the cards left in other hands" (the default), or "Everyone scores their own cards; lowest wins": after each hand, every player adds up the cards left in their own hand, and when someone reaches the target, the lowest score wins.
- **When you can't play:** "Draw until you can play" (the default); "Draw one card; play it if you can"; or "Draw up to 3 cards, then pass".
- **Reshuffle the discard pile when the stock runs out** (off by default): all but the top card of the discard pile is shuffled into a new stock.
- **Cards dealt:** "7 for two players, 5 for more" (the default), "7 each", or "8 each".
- **If the starting card is an 8:** "Put it back and turn another" (the default), or "It counts as a normal card".
- **2s: the next player draws 2 and misses a turn** (off by default).
- **2s stack** (off by default, needs the rule above): instead of drawing, the next player may play a 2 of their own to pass the draw on. Each 2 adds 2 more cards, and the first player who doesn't play a 2 draws them all.
- **Jacks skip the next player** (off by default).
- **Aces reverse direction** (off by default). With two players, an ace works as a skip.
- **Queen of spades: the next player draws 5** (off by default), and misses their turn.
