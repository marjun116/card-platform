# Cribbage

Score points for combinations of cards, first as you play them one by one, then by counting your hand. First to 121 wins.

## Players and cards

- 2 players, or 3 playing alone, or 4 as two partnerships (partners sit opposite).
- One standard deck. Aces are low. For counting, face cards are worth 10, aces 1, and other cards their number.
- With 2 players each gets 6 cards; with 3 or 4, 5 cards each.
- The player who isn't the dealer is the **pone**.

## The crib

Each player puts cards face down into the **crib**, which belongs to the dealer, until they're holding 4. With 3 players, one card from the deck goes in as well, so the crib always has 4 cards.

## The starter

The dealer turns up the next card of the deck, the **starter**. It counts as part of every hand and the crib. If the starter is a jack, the dealer pegs 2 points (**his heels**).

## The play

Starting with the player left of the dealer, players take turns laying one card face up in front of them and saying the running total. The total can't go past 31.

You score as you play:

- **Fifteen:** bring the total to exactly 15: 2 points.
- **Thirty-one:** bring it to exactly 31: 2 points.
- **Pairs:** play a card of the same rank as the card just played: 2 points. Three in a row of the same rank: 6. Four: 12.
- **Runs:** if your card and the cards just before it make a run of 3 or more (in any order), 1 point for each card in the run.
- **Go:** if you can't play without going past 31, you're passed over. When nobody can play, whoever played the last card scores 1 (or 2 for exactly 31), and the total starts again from 0.
- **Last card:** playing the very last card of the play scores 1.

Going and passing are automatic here: if you can't play, the table skips you.

## The show

After the play, each hand is counted along with the starter: the pone's hand first, then the dealer's, then the crib (which the dealer scores). Press **Count my hand** when it's your turn, so everyone sees each count.

- **Fifteens:** every combination of cards adding up to 15: 2 points each.
- **Pairs:** every pair of the same rank: 2 points each (three of a kind is 3 pairs, 6 points).
- **Runs:** 3 or more cards in order: 1 point per card. Each different way of making the run counts, so 3-3-4-5 is two runs of three.
- **Flush:** all 4 cards in hand the same suit: 4 points, or 5 if the starter matches too. The crib only scores a flush when all 5 cards match.
- **Nobs:** the jack of the same suit as the starter: 1 point.

## Winning

The first player (or side) to reach 121 wins at once, even in the middle of a hand.

A loser who finished under 91 is **skunked**, and under 61 **double skunked**. That's shown with the result, but isn't counted as an extra win.

## House rules

- **Play to:** 121 (the default) or 61. When playing to 61, a skunk means finishing under 31.
- **Five-card cribbage** (off by default, two players): each player gets 5 cards and keeps 3, and the pone pegs 3 points at the start of each hand. Usually played to 61.
- **A four-card flush counts in the crib** (off by default).
- **A flush in hand only counts if the starter matches too** (off by default).
- **His heels** (on by default): turn it off and a jack starter scores nothing.
