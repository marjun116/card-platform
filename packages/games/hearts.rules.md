# Hearts

A trick-taking game where you try not to win points. Every heart is worth a point and the queen of spades is worth 13. Lowest score wins.

## Players and cards

- 3, 4 or 5 players, each playing alone.
- One standard deck. Aces are high. There is no trump suit.
- With 3 players the 2♦ is taken out (17 cards each). With 5 players the 2♦ and 2♠ are taken out (10 cards each). With 4 players everyone gets 13.

## Object

Take as few points as possible. The game ends when someone reaches the target score (100 unless the table chose otherwise), and the lowest score wins.

## Passing

Before each hand, everyone passes 3 cards face down to another player. You choose your 3 cards before you see what's passed to you.

- With 4 players the direction goes left, right, across, then a hand with no passing (a "hold" hand), and repeats.
- With 3 or 5 players it goes left, right, then hold.

## Play

- The player holding the 2♣ leads it to the first trick.
- You must follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play any card.
- The highest card of the suit led wins the trick, and the winner leads the next one.
- **No points on the first trick:** you can't play a heart or the queen of spades on the first trick, unless your hand holds nothing else.
- **Hearts must be broken:** you can't lead a heart until a heart has been played on an earlier trick, unless your hand holds only hearts. Playing the queen of spades doesn't break hearts.

## Scoring

At the end of each hand, each player scores the points in the tricks they took:

- 1 point for each heart.
- 13 points for the queen of spades.

**Shooting the moon:** if one player takes all 13 hearts and the queen of spades (all 26 points), they score 0 and every other player scores 26.

## Winning

When anyone reaches the target score at the end of a hand, the game ends and the lowest score wins. If two or more players are tied for lowest, another hand is played.

## House rules

- **Play to:** the score that ends the game: 50, 75, 100 (the default) or 150.
- **Shooting the moon:** what happens when one player takes all 26 points: "Everyone else gets 26" (the default); "The shooter takes 26 off", so the shooter's score goes down by 26 instead; or "The shooter chooses" which of those two happens.
- **Jack of diamonds is worth -10** (off by default): whoever takes the J♦ takes 10 points off their score. It doesn't count toward shooting the moon, which still means taking all the hearts and the queen of spades.
- **Allow points on the first trick** (off by default): hearts and the queen of spades may be played on the first trick.
- **Queen of spades breaks hearts** (off by default): once the queen of spades has been played, hearts may be led.
- **Hearts may be led any time** (off by default): there's no need to wait for hearts to be broken.
- **Passing:** "Left, right, across, hold" (the default); "Left, right, across (no hold)"; "Always left"; or "No passing". With 3 or 5 players there is no "across" pass.
- **First lead:** "2 of clubs leads" (the default), or "Left of the dealer leads any card".
- **Tied winners share the win** (off by default): when players tie for the lowest score at the end, they all win instead of playing another hand.
